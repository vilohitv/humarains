The short version

On WhatsApp, replies to customers who message you go out automatically. You choose whether email replies wait for your approval. 2What the Service does

Humarains is an AI agent that helps small service businesses such as tradespeople, cleaners, landscapers and contractors handle enquiries and bookings. It reads new enquiries from your connected email inbox, WhatsApp Business number or website form, and replies with quotes taken from the services and prices you set. It follows up when a customer goes quiet, books jobs in your Google Calendar, and hands a conversation to you when a person is needed.

It also includes an Agent you can chat with to run your account, an Agent that suggests follow-ups and sends you a morning briefing and alerts, workflows you can build, campaigns to groups of people, a knowledge base and photo library, and, on the Pro plan, lead scoring and deal insights. When a customer sends photos on WhatsApp, the Service can analyse them to prepare an estimate from your prices.

How replies go out depends on your plan and settings. On email, replies wait for your approval or are sent automatically, as you choose (the Free plan always waits for approval). On WhatsApp, the Service replies to customers who message you automatically and promptly, without waiting for your approval. You can read every conversation in your dashboard and disconnect at any time. Results such as accepted quotes or booked jobs depend on many things outside our control, and we do not promise any.

5Email connection

If you connect an email account you allow Humarains to:

  • read new, unread messages in your inbox over IMAP to find customer enquiries

Your credentials are stored encrypted and deleted when you disconnect. You can disconnect at any time from your dashboard.

6WhatsApp connection

If your plan includes WhatsApp and you connect a WhatsApp Business account through Meta’s official Cloud API, you allow Humarains to:

  • receive messages sent to your business number and turn them into leads
  • reply to customers who message you, automatically and without prior review, within 24 hours of their last message
  • download and analyse photos customers send, to prepare an estimate from your prices

The Service only replies on WhatsApp. It never starts a conversation, never sends message templates, marketing or broadcast messages, replies only to people who messaged your business first and only within WhatsApp’s 24-hour window, and stops when someone opts out. Follow-ups after that window go by email when the customer gave an address.

You are responsible for your own Meta Business account, for following Meta’s WhatsApp Business Policy and messaging rules, and for any consent from customers that the law requires. Meta alone decides whether a number is restricted or banned, and we are not responsible for those decisions. Because replies go out automatically, you are responsible for keeping your services, prices and quoting rules accurate, for reading your conversations, and for telling customers, where the law requires, that they are talking to an automated assistant. You can disconnect WhatsApp at any time, which deletes the stored credentials.
