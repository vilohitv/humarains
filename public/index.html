Questions owners ask

Will it send things I have not seen?

Routine replies go out on their own, so customers are not left waiting. When something needs a person, such as a complaint, an unusual request or a job above your size limit, Humarains stops, holds the conversation in Review, and waits for you. You can also pause the AI on any chat and reply yourself from the Inbox.

How does it know my prices?

You enter your services, rates and quoting rules in Business Settings. It quotes only from those. If a request does not fit them, it hands the lead to you instead of guessing.

Where do my enquiries come from?

You connect your email inbox and your WhatsApp Business number, and you can send in enquiries from your website form. They all land in one place.

What happens when the AI is not sure?

It stops and flags the enquiry for you, with the reason, for example a complaint, a very large job, or missing details. Nothing is sent until a person has looked.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time from your billing page. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for, and your enquiries and settings are kept if you come back.
