Human handover
When a customer is ready to book, the AI stops replying and passes the conversation to the right person on your team, with the customer's phone number and email. The AI tells the customer who will be in touch.
Hand over when
Your team
List the jobs each person handles (for example "roof repair, gutters"). The AI matches the customer's job to a person. Someone marked "everything else" gets leads that match nobody. If there is no match and no such person, the handover comes to you.
What your Agent can do on its own
Choose what the Agent may do without you: follow up with quiet leads, offer a discount, text back a missed WhatsApp call, ask for reviews. For each one you decide whether it is off, asks you first, or runs automatically. It never goes beyond the limits you set here.
Time zone, hours & scheduling
Tell us your time zone and when you are open. The AI uses these to say when you are available, and to word replies differently when you are closed.

Quote Rules
Tell the AI when it may give a number and when it must ask for more detail or book a visit first. This is what stops under-quoting and over-promising.
Photo Quotes
When a customer sends photos and asks for a price, the AI looks at them, works out what the job is and roughly how big it is, and gives an estimate using your services, pricing and quote rules above. It always says the estimate is based on photos.
Payment, Warranty & Policies
The terms customers ask about right before they say yes. The AI states these accurately instead of improvising.
