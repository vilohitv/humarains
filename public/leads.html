Replies and follow-ups
Humarains only replies on WhatsApp. It answers people who message your number, and only within 24 hours of their last message, which is the window WhatsApp allows for free-form replies. Connect Your Email
One account, two protocols: IMAP reads inbound enquiries, SMTP sends AI replies. Most providers (Gmail, Outlook, Zoho, your own domain host, etc.) support both with the same email + password or app password.
How to connect your inbox — step by step
Quick-fill for common providers
Part 1 — Gmail (App Password)
Gmail requires a 16-character App Password — your normal Google password will not work. Follow these steps to generate one.
1
Go to myaccount.google.com/security and make sure 2-Step Verification is turned On. App Passwords are only available when 2FA is enabled.
2
Visit myaccount.google.com/apppasswords. In the App name field type Humarains and click Create.
3
Google displays a 16-character password (e.g. abcd efgh ijkl mnop). Copy it, paste it into the Password / App Password field below (spaces are fine), and click Gmail above to fill in the server settings automatically.
Part 2 — Outlook / Microsoft 365
4
Sign into account.microsoft.com. Go to Security → Advanced security options → App passwords and create a new one named Humarains.
5
If your account is managed by an organisation (Microsoft 365), ask your IT admin to enable SMTP AUTH for your mailbox in the Microsoft 365 Admin Center under Users → Active users → [your account] → Mail → Manage email apps.
6
Click Outlook / Microsoft 365 above to auto-fill the server fields, enter your email address and the app password, then click Connect Email.
Part 3 — Any other provider (Zoho, cPanel, custom domain, etc.)
7
Find your provider's IMAP and SMTP settings — usually in their help centre under "Email client settings" or "IMAP configuration". You need four values: IMAP host, IMAP port (usually 993), SMTP host, and SMTP port (usually 587).
8
Use your normal email password unless your provider requires an app-specific password (Zoho does — generate one at accounts.zoho.com → Security → App Passwords).
9
Fill in all fields below and click Connect Email. Humarains verifies the IMAP connection before saving — if it fails, double-check the hostname and that IMAP is enabled on your account.
Once connected, Humarains polls your inbox automatically every few minutes and processes new enquiries with AI — no manual action needed.
